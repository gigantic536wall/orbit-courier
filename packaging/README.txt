ORBIT COURIER
=============

Deliver parcels between stations orbiting planets and moons. The engine is weak and fuel
is scarce, so use gravity: fall towards a planet to speed up and let orbits do the work.

Dock by getting close to the target station at a low relative speed (the HUD turns green).
The blue line shows where you will coast; the marker on it shows your closest approach to
the target.

Controls
  A / D or Left / Right   turn
  W, Up or Space          thrust
  Shift (held)            fine thrust for docking
  Tab                     system map
  Mouse wheel             zoom
  P / Esc                 pause
  R                       restart the level
  M                       sound on / off

Your progress is saved in orbit-courier-progress.txt next to the game.

macOS: the app is not signed. If macOS refuses to open it, right-click the file and choose
Open, or run:  xattr -d com.apple.quarantine orbit_courier
Linux: needs OpenGL 3.3 and X11 (or XWayland).
